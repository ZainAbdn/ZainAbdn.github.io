Approach

Forward deployed: close to the problem, accountable for the outcome.

I don't work from a ticket queue. I sit with the people who own the problem, build in their environment, and stay until the system is running and trusted.

  1. 01

    Embed & discover

    Work alongside operators and leadership to map the workflow, the data and the risk, and agree what “done” means in business terms.

  2. 02

    Prototype fast

    Turn the first conversation into a working proof within days, using Python, SQL, APIs or Power Platform, so feedback comes from something real.

  3. 03

    Ship securely

    Harden it for production: least-privilege access, logging, controlled change and evidence that maps to ISO 27001 and NIST RMF.

  4. 04

    Operate & hand over

    Measure the impact, train the team, document the run-book, and feed what I learned back into the product and roadmap.
