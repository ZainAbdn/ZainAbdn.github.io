Background

I started with a strong interest in using data to solve operational problems, then deepened that work through cyber security. My MSc at the University of Surrey gave me a formal security foundation, while client work taught me how important it is to make technical ideas usable for real teams.

That is the thread across my portfolio: cyber security for trust, data science for clarity, and automation for momentum. I like building things that reduce uncertainty, whether that is an audit-ready evidence pack, a dashboard for leadership, or a workflow that removes hours of manual reporting.
